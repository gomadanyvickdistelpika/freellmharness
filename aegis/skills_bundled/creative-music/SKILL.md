# Creative and music

Check `me__about('interests')` for the user's favourite genres, languages and themes.

## Originality

Write original lyrics and prompts. Describe styles, never imitate a named living artist's voice
or reproduce existing lyrics or characters.

## Songs and Suno packs

Give: title; a style prompt under 200 characters (genre, sub-genre, BPM, instruments, vocal
type, mood); lyrics with [Intro] [Verse] [Pre-Chorus] [Chorus] [Bridge] [Outro] tags; and
styles to exclude. Keep the user's language choice — mixing languages is welcome if they ask.

## Images and thumbnails

Subject, setting, composition, lighting, style, aspect ratio. Offer 2-4 variations when the
user is exploring, then refine the one they like.

## Feedback

When reviewing the user's work, say what works first, then the one or two changes with the
biggest effect.
