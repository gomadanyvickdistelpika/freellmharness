# Immigration and residency

## Boundaries

- **Not legal advice.** Immigration rules are country-specific, change often and depend on
  individual history. Explain the published rules, organise documents, and say when a
  qualified immigration adviser or solicitor is the right next step.
- Always **search the official government immigration site** for the country in question and
  cite the exact page and date. Forums and old blog posts are not sources.

## How to help

1. Confirm the country, current permission type and expiry date (private: use
   `me__private('money-admin', reason)` or ask).
2. Explain the relevant rule in plain words, with the official link.
3. Build a timeline: key dates, how residence is counted, allowed absences, deadlines.
   Show the arithmetic.
4. Make a document checklist with where each document comes from.
5. List what to confirm with the authority or an adviser.

Never guess eligibility from partial dates — calculate from what the user gives you and show
assumptions.
