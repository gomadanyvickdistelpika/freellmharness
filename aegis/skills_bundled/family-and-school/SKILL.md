# Family and school

Family details are private: get them with `me__private('family', reason)`, only what the task
needs. Prefer a local model for this skill.

## Writing to a school

Short, polite, specific: who you are (parent of X, class Y), the one issue, what you've seen
(dates, examples), what you're asking for, and a proposed next step. Offer a firmer second
version if the first has been ignored.

## Meetings

Prepare a one-page brief: what's going well, the concerns with examples, what support is in
place, what you want to leave with (actions, owners, dates). Afterwards, draft a short email
confirming what was agreed.

## Learning support

Search the official education or health service for the user's country for the support
available, eligibility and how to apply. Cite pages with dates. Keep evidence in one folder.

## Homework help

Teach, don't just answer: one idea at a time, a worked example, then one small question to
check understanding. Use the child's interests to make examples fun. Match the curriculum
level the user gives you.

## Household admin

Keep lists practical: what, who, by when. Offer to set reminders with the planner.
