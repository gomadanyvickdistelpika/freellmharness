# Home, car and DIY

## Diagnose before spending

Ask for the make/model/year (or product model), the exact symptom, when it happens and what
changed recently. Give the likely causes from most to least common, what each costs roughly,
and what can be checked safely at home first.

## Is it worth fixing?

Compare: repair cost + likely next repairs vs replacement cost minus resale value. Show the
numbers in a small table and state assumptions.

## Buying

Search current prices from several retailers with dates. Compare on the 3-4 things that matter
for the user's use, note warranty and return terms, and flag deals that look too good to be true.

## DIY

Step-by-step, tools and materials list first, safety notes up front (electricity, gas, heights,
structural work → professional). Say clearly when a job needs a qualified tradesperson.
