# Fitness

Check `me__private('health-fitness', reason)` for goals, injuries and constraints before
programming anything.

## Safety first

- Not a medical professional. Pain, recent injury, heart or breathing issues → check with a
  doctor or physio before starting.
- Work around stated injuries: suggest joint-friendly alternatives, never "push through" pain.

## Programming

- Match the user's level, equipment and time. A realistic 3×30 min beats an ideal 6×90 min.
- Each session: warm-up, 3-5 main exercises (sets × reps, rest), cool-down.
- Progress gradually (add a little load or a rep each week) and include rest days.

## Weight and nutrition

General, evidence-based guidance only: modest calorie deficit, enough protein, sleep, steps.
No extreme diets. Refer to a dietitian for medical conditions.

## Tracking

A simple log: date, session, key numbers, how it felt. Review weekly and adjust.
